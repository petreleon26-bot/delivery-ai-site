How Delivery AI works

A simple flow that fits into the delivery operation the company already uses.

01

Delivery created

The company's system sends the delivery data to Delivery AI.

02

Courier approaches

ETA is updated as the courier gets closer to the customer.

03

AI calls customer

The call can trigger automatically at the configured ETA threshold.

04

AI understands

The system extracts the useful delivery instructions from the conversation.

05

Courier receives it

The information appears live in the courier interface in the courier's language.
